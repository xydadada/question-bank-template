# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
publication of private information and deliberate disruption are not accepted.

Keep technical discussions focused on reproducible behavior. Never post another
person's credentials, documents, knowledge-base contents or identifying logs.
Security reports belong in GitHub's private security-advisory channel.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to
protect contributors or the project. Good-faith technical disagreement is
welcome; personal attacks are not.
